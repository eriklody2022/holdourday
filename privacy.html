Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

Hold Our Day (holdourday.com) helps couples check wedding vendor availability, place tentative holds and request meetings. This page explains what information we collect, who can see it and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Your Google sign-in. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • What you put in a hold. Your names, email, phone number (optional), expected guest count, the dates you pick, the meeting times you suggest and any notes you write.
  • What happens to a hold. Its status (tentative hold, meeting set, booked or released), the meeting time a vendor sets and when it was placed or updated.

Browsing the site does not require an account. We don't run advertising trackers or sell ads.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own holds and bookings under My plan.
  • The Hold Our Day vendor desk can see your holds, including your contact details, so it can arrange meetings and confirm bookings with the venue or vendor you chose.
  • Other visitors only see that a date is on hold or booked. They never see your name, email, phone number or notes.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone except to arrange the meetings and bookings you ask for, or when the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Sign-in and hold information is stored with Google Firebase, a service run by Google. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. Like most web services, these providers may keep basic technical logs such as IP addresses. Their own privacy policies cover that data.

Your browser also stores a few things on your device: your sign-in session, so you stay signed in, and your search filters, so they're remembered next visit. You can clear these by signing out or clearing your browser's site data.

How long we keep it

Tentative holds expire on their own if no meeting is set. We keep a limited history of your past holds and bookings so you can see them in My plan. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

  • Release any hold yourself, or ask the vendor desk to release it.
  • Ask us what information we have about you, to correct it or to delete it and your account.

Email erik.lodermeier@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can.

Children

Hold Our Day is meant for adults planning a wedding. It isn't directed at anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
